# Sneakersquad

Build a Professional E-Commerce Website for Sneaker Squad



1. Project Overview



Create a modern, premium, visually impressive and mobile-friendly website for SNEAKER SQUAD, a South African online retail business selling sneakers, iPhones, clothing, gaming products and accessories.



The website must look like a professional online store built by an experienced web designer. It should be clean, organised, fast, easy to navigate and designed to turn visitors into customers.



Official business name: Sneaker Squad



Use “Sneaker Squad” consistently throughout the entire website, including the logo text, navigation, homepage, product pages, About Us, contact section, footer, page titles and website metadata.



Never use “THEBOYINNO iPhone Store” as the business name.



WhatsApp ordering number: +27 68 211 5106



Primary goal: Help customers browse products, check listed prices, select their preferred products and contact Sneaker Squad directly through WhatsApp to enquire or place an order.



For the first version, use WhatsApp enquiries instead of a complicated online payment system. Do not pretend that an order or payment has been completed on the website.



2. Brand Identity and Visual Design



Create a premium streetwear and technology retail aesthetic.



Colours



- Primary background: deep navy and near-black.

- Secondary background: dark charcoal.

- Accent colour: electric blue or cyan.

- Main text: white.

- Secondary text: soft grey.

- Buttons: electric blue with clear white text.



Use the colours consistently and avoid making the website too colourful or cluttered.



Typography



Use modern, bold headings and clean, readable body text. Product names and prices must be easy to read on mobile phones.



Logo



Create a simple text-based logo that reads SNEAKER SQUAD. A subtle sneaker-inspired or shopping-inspired symbol may accompany it.



Do not copy another company's logo or use an existing brand's identity as the Sneaker Squad logo.



Overall appearance



- Premium, modern and trustworthy.

- Strong product photography.

- Consistent spacing and alignment.

- Rounded product cards with subtle borders.

- Clear calls to action.

- Professional navigation and footer.

- No unnecessary visual clutter.

- No excessive animations or distracting effects.



3. Animations and Interactions



Add subtle, premium animations to make the website feel modern.



Include:



- Smooth scrolling between sections.

- Gentle fade-in and upward movement as sections enter the viewport.

- Subtle hover effects on product cards.

- Smooth transitions when buttons are hovered over or clicked.

- Small scale effects on product images when hovered over on desktop.

- A refined hero-section entrance animation.

- Smooth mobile navigation transitions.

- A clear visual response when a customer adds a product to their enquiry list.



Animations must be lightweight, fast and suitable for mobile devices.



Respect reduced-motion accessibility settings. Do not add heavy video backgrounds, excessive parallax, or animations that make the website slow.

5. Homepage



Create an attractive homepage with the following sections.



Hero section



Use a strong headline:



YOUR STYLE. YOUR TECH. YOUR SQUAD.



Supporting text:



“Discover sneakers, iPhones, streetwear and everyday tech essentials at Sneaker Squad. Browse our collection and contact us on WhatsApp to enquire about your next purchase.”



Primary button: Shop Collection



Secondary button: Chat on WhatsApp



Use a high-quality, relevant hero image featuring sneakers and technology products. Avoid a crowded collage. Make sure the text remains readable over the image.



Do not claim that products are authentic, brand-new, sealed or officially guaranteed unless the owner has confirmed these details.



Shop by category



Create attractive visual category cards for:



- iPhones

- Sneakers

- Clothing

- Gaming

- Accessories



Each card must lead to the correct product category.



Featured products



Display a selection of products with:



- Product image.

- Product name.

- Price in South African rand.

- Relevant size or storage information where available.

- An enquiry or WhatsApp button.



Only show prices and product details supplied or confirmed by the business. Where the price is missing, display “Price on enquiry”.



Why shop with Sneaker Squad?



Use a clean section highlighting:



- A range of lifestyle and technology products.

- Convenient WhatsApp enquiries.

- Delivery options to be confirmed with the business.

- Personal assistance with product enquiries.



Do not invent customer reviews, sales numbers, awards, guarantees or delivery promises.



Final call to action



Headline: FOUND SOMETHING YOU LIKE?



Text: “Contact Sneaker Squad on WhatsApp to check availability, confirm the price and discuss delivery.”



Button: Chat With Us

Every product card should support:

- Category.

- Price in rand.

- Storage capacity or available sizes, when known.

- Availability status, only when confirmed.

- WhatsApp enquiry button.



Include search and category filters. Where practical, allow sorting by price.



Do not invent missing prices, stock levels, product specifications or sizes. If information is uncertain or conflicting, mark it for the owner to confirm rather than presenting it as fact.

7. WhatsApp Ordering System



WhatsApp is the main ordering and enquiry channel for the first version.



Use this WhatsApp number everywhere it is needed:



+27 68 211 5106

https://wa.me/27682115106



Each product should have a working WhatsApp enquiry button.



The button must open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message such as:



“Hi Sneaker Squad, I am interested in [product name]. Please confirm the current price, availability and delivery options.”



When the customer selects a product size or iPhone storage variant, include that selection in the message where possible.



If practical, allow customers to select multiple products into an enquiry list and then send the list through WhatsApp. If this feature is included, make sure it works properly on mobile and desktop.



Important:



- Do not display a false checkout-success message.

- Do not collect payment details.

- Do not pretend that WhatsApp messages have been sent if the customer has not completed the action in WhatsApp.

- Show a clear message explaining that the customer must confirm availability, final pricing and delivery with the business.

Our aim is to make it easy for customers to explore our products and contact us directly for enquiries and orders. Browse the collection, choose what interests you and speak to our team on WhatsApp for assistance.”



Do not invent the founding date, owner biography, physical shop location, company registration details or business history.



9. Contact and Delivery Information



Display a professional contact page with:



- Business name: Sneaker Squad.

- WhatsApp: +27 68 211 5106.

- A prominent WhatsApp contact button.

- A short enquiry form only if it can work correctly and securely.

- A delivery information section.

Present delivery methods as options to confirm with the business, not as guaranteed services. A supplied listing mentions an estimated Paxi delivery period of approximately 3–5 days, but the owner must confirm the current timeframe, applicable areas and fees before this is published as a promise.



Do not invent a physical address, email address, collection point, delivery fee or guaranteed delivery date.



10. Returns and Lay-by



The supplied business profile states “No refunds. Hustle Returns Allowed” and some product listings mention lay-by.



Do not automatically publish these phrases as final policy wording.



Create a section that can be completed after the owner confirms:



- The applicable returns and refund policy.

- What “Hustle Returns Allowed” means in practice.

- Whether lay-by is available.

- Any deposit, payment schedule, cancellation and collection terms.



Do not invent terms or promise refunds, exchanges or lay-by conditions that have not been confirmed. Ensure the final wording complies with applicable South African consumer-protection requirements.



11. Product Images



Use the product photos supplied by the business wherever possible.



If more images are needed:



- Use appropriate, high-quality images that the business has permission to use.

- Use relevant placeholder images during development.

- Avoid unrelated products and misleading product images.

- Never use a different phone model or sneaker design to represent a product.

- Make it easy to replace images when the owner provides the correct photos.



Use consistent image ratios and crop product images neatly without cutting off important details.



12. Owner Product Management



Structure the website so products can be updated without redesigning every page.



Each product should have editable fields for:



- Name.

- Category.

- Price.

- Image.

- Description.

- Storage capacity or available sizes.

- Availability status.

- Featured status.



For the first demo, a simple catalogue is acceptable.



If the owner needs to add products, change prices or update stock independently, implement a genuine product-management system with persistent data storage and secure owner authentication. Do not create a fake admin dashboard that only changes temporary data in the browser.



Do not expose secret keys or administrative credentials in public code.



If a backend or database is required, explain the required setup before claiming that product management is fully operational.



13. Footer



Create a clean footer containing:



- Sneaker Squad branding.

- Links to the main pages and product categories.

- WhatsApp contact.

- Delivery and returns information links, once confirmed.

- A copyright notice with a suitable current year.

- Social media links only when the correct profiles are provided.



Do not invent social media URLs or contact information.



14. Technical and Quality Requirements



- Make the website responsive on mobile, tablet and desktop.

- Prioritise the mobile shopping experience.

- Use reusable product cards and components.

- Ensure every navigation link and button works.

- Use accessible colour contrast and readable text.

- Add descriptive image alt text.

- Use SEO-friendly page titles and descriptions.

- Optimise image sizes and page loading speed.

- Provide loading, empty and error states where appropriate.

- Ensure product filters and search actually function if displayed.

- Use clean, maintainable code.

- Avoid unnecessary dependencies and broken placeholder links.

- Do not add fake reviews, fake discounts, fake stock counters or unsupported trust badges.

- Do not claim that online payments, stock management or order tracking work unless they are genuinely implemented and tested.



15. Final Result



Deliver a complete, polished website for SNEAKER SQUAD with a premium dark-and-electric-blue visual identity, subtle animations, a well-organised catalogue, clear South African rand pricing, and working WhatsApp enquiries.



Make the website look professional enough to present to a real business owner as a client project.



Before treating the website as ready for public launch, clearly identify all information that still needs confirmation from the owner, especially prices, product availability, product condition, delivery fees and timeframes, returns, lay-by and the correct product images.



Build the website now. Do not stop at a wireframe or a generic description. Implement the actual pages, styling, responsive layout and working interactions.
